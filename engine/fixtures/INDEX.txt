casting-hall.json — the Ferro & Lume casting hall.

A fictional worked example, invented for this planner and not a survey of any
real building. 12 by 9 cells: three casting runs (North, Main, South) painted
across the width, a dispatch apron along the south wall, and four aisles
(West, Two, Three, Four) cut through the racks between them. First paint wins,
so the runs own their rows and the aisles own the rack gaps.

Six tasks ride the floor: two express runs, two standard runs, two bulk runs.
Dues are tight on purpose — Tinder's run (T-03) is the one the prioritized
rung cannot serve once the other traffic is booked, which is what the
deadline-hit-rate curve and the degradation ladder report.

Two window asks ride in the document (W-1 Kiln, W-2 Ash) so the release-gate
walkthrough is data, not code.

Provenance: hand-authored for the hearthline planner. Sources for the method
itself are cited in the repository README (Sharon et al. 2015; openTCS and
Open-RMF as industry analogs). The hall, the tasks, and every figure are
fictional.
